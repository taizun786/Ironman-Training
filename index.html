Sync setup

Your ticks and edits are always saved on this device first. To share them across devices, mirror them to a JSONBin bin. Create a new bin just for this calendar (don't reuse The Workbench's) at jsonbin.io with the content {"app":"training-tracker"}, then paste its Bin ID and your Master Key here.

The key is stored only in this browser's local storage on this device, never in the bin or the page.

Text export / import

Export gives you the whole plan as plain text (with each item's [id]) — a backup you can paste anywhere. Paste edited text into Import to change content without redeploying.
